
# Tata Data Visualisation Simulation | Power BI Business Insights Project

Completed Tata’s Data Visualisation virtual experience program on Forage using Power BI and Excel. 
This project focuses on transforming raw business data into meaningful visual insights to support business<
decision-making through interactive dashboards and data storytelling.

The project helped me understand how organizations use data analytics and visualisation to solve business problems,
identify trends, and support strategic decisions for senior management such as the CEO and CMO.

---

## About the Project

This simulation involved:
- Understanding business requirements from leadership teams
- Selecting suitable charts and visuals for different scenarios
- Cleaning and preparing raw business data
- Building interactive dashboards in Power BI
- Communicating insights through data storytelling

The dashboards were designed to answer business-related questions using visual analytics and trend analysis.

# Dashboard Insights

## Dashboard 1 – Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis

![Dashboard 1](project/dashboard1.png)

### Objective
To analyze monthly revenue trends for the year 2011 and help leadership identify performance patterns across different months and countries.

### Insights
- Used filters to analyze country-specific monthly performance
- Created a revenue trend line chart for better visualization of growth patterns
- Added revenue tables for clear understanding of monthly performance
- Helped identify high and low revenue periods during the year
